Admission Process

Step 1

Submit Application

Complete the online registration form and provide your child’s basic personal details.

Step 2

Review & Interview

Our school team reviews the application and may contact you for an interview or follow-up.

Step 3

Enrollment

Once approved, you receive enrollment information, school fees details, and orientation guidance.

Requirements

Documents needed

  • Birth certificate or proof of age
  • Previous school records or report cards
  • Parent or guardian ID
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Completed application form
Eligibility

Who can apply?

  • Students seeking admission into Grades 9–12
  • New and transfer learners
  • Parents looking for a values-based learning community
  • Students ready to grow through academics and leadership
